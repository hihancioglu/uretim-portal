# WP-008 Deferred Decisions

WP-008 deliberately does not decide or implement:

- mold-based automatic eye count;
- a required visual-control catalog;
- tickets or ticket behavior after correction;
- metrology or commissioning;
- legacy measurement migration;
- SPC;
- deletion/retention workflows;
- historical limit correction.

These remain gates for their approved future work packages. No speculative constraint, child deletion, reopen behavior, or ticket side effect was introduced.
